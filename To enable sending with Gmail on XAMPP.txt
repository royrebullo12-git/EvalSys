To enable sending with Gmail on XAMPP:

1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for the Gmail account that will send the messages, then create a Google App Password. Use that app password—not your regular Gmail password.

2. Open C:\xampp\apache\conf\httpd.conf and add these lines near the bottom, replacing the example addresses and password with your own values:

SetEnv EVALSYS_SMTP_HOST smtp.gmail.com
SetEnv EVALSYS_SMTP_PORT 587
SetEnv EVALSYS_SMTP_SECURE tls
SetEnv EVALSYS_SMTP_USERNAME your-sender@gmail.com
SetEnv EVALSYS_SMTP_PASSWORD your-16-character-app-password
SetEnv EVALSYS_SMTP_FROM_EMAIL your-sender@gmail.com
SetEnv EVALSYS_SMTP_FROM_NAME EvalSys

Keep the app password private; don’t put it in chat or commit it to the project.

3. Stop and restart Apache in the XAMPP Control Panel so it picks up the settings.

4. Open the app through XAMPP at http://localhost/EvalSys.v3.3%20-%20With%20Profile/login.html (not as a file:/// page), choose Forgot password?, enter the employee’s saved email address, and click Send Verification Code.

5. Check that mailbox’s inbox and spam folder. Enter the six-digit code, choose a new password of at least 8 characters, then sign in with it.
